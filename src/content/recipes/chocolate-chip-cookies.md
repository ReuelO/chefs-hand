# Chocolate Chip Cookies

These classic chocolate chip cookies are perfect for any occasion. They're easy to make and always a crowd favorite.

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).

2. In a bowl, whisk together flour, baking soda, and salt.

3. In another bowl, cream together butter and sugars until light and fluffy.

4. Beat in eggs one at a time, then stir in vanilla.

5. Gradually blend in flour mixture.

6. Fold in chocolate chips.

7. Drop rounded tablespoons of dough onto ungreased cookie sheets.

8. Bake for 9-11 minutes or until golden brown.

9. Cool on baking sheet for 2 minutes before removing to wire rack.
